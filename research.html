Working Paper

Testing for Structural Changes in Factor-Augmented Quantile Regression via Discrete Fourier Transform

with Xia Wang and Lingyun Zhou

Fourier-based tests for structural changes in factor-augmented quantile regression, with applications to U.S. macroeconomic panels.

Research summary

This project develops tests that allow for general forms of coefficient instability at a given conditional quantile. It establishes asymptotic properties and bootstrap validity with PCA-estimated latent factors, evaluates finite-sample size and power using Monte Carlo simulations, and examines structural-change patterns across frequencies in monthly and quarterly U.S. macroeconomic panels.
